# Adams Resources & Energy

> Adams Resources & Energy, Inc. (NYSE American: AE) is a diversified energy company engaged in the marketing, transportation, terminalling and storage of crude oil and other related petroleum products in select markets across the United States. It operates through the subsidiaries GulfMark Energy (crude oil marketing), Service Transport Company and Firebird Bulk Carriers (bulk liquid and chemical transportation), Phoenix Oil (off-spec fuel recycling and repurposing) and Victoria Express Pipeline (pipeline operations).

## Business

- Crude Oil Marketing: purchases and markets crude oil at the wellhead across the Gulf Coast, Eagle Ford Shale, Permian Basin, Bakken Shale and Michigan basins.
- Crude Oil Transportation: a fleet of tractor-trailers moving crude oil and petroleum products.
- Terminalling and Storage: dock facilities and pipeline inventory locations.
- Specialty Chemical Transportation: bulk liquid chemical and dry bulk commodity hauling.
- Off-Spec Fuel Recycling: recycling and repurposing of off-spec fuels, lubricants and chemicals.
- Pipeline Operations: crude oil transportation and delivery infrastructure.
